# Cabin Week on iPhone and iPad

No App Store app is needed: the game works as a web app. On the home screen it has its own icon, starts full screen and also works offline. Free.

## Install (once)

The game shows a hint with these steps. This is how:

1. Open **Safari** (not Chrome) and go to **https://cabinweek.online/**
2. Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow up, at the bottom or top).
3. Scroll and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**. The **Cabin Week** icon is now on the home screen.

From now on, open the game with that icon. Your progress stays on the device.

## Playing

- **Turn the device sideways:** you see more of the level, and the menu fits on one screen.
- **Buttons on the screen:** ◀ ▶ walk (double-tap and hold to run), ⤒ jump, 💬 talk. Walk and tap 💬 when nobody is near to belly-slide.
- **Sound** starts with the first tap. Hear nothing? Turn off silent mode and tap 🔊 in the top bar.
- **A controller** (PlayStation, Xbox) also works: pair it in Settings → Bluetooth, open the game and press a button.
- **On the TV:** with **AirPlay** (Control Centre → Screen Mirroring) on an Apple TV or a TV with AirPlay.

## A new version

The game updates itself. When "A new version is ready" appears at the bottom, tap **Reload**.
