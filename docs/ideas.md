# Ideas for future regions

The grey dots on the map ("coming later"). Nothing here is built yet. When a region gets built, move its ideas into the code and AGENTS.md, and remove it from `SOON` in `js/regions.js`.

Every region follows the same recipe as the others (see AGENTS.md, "Regions" and "Region gameplay"):
- **eight days**, one level a day, with its own progress;
- a **fictional village or camping** where the families stay, at real places they visit;
- **its own gameplay**, not just another background: scenery (`scene`), ground (`theme`), creatures, decorations, and at least one thing that plays differently;
- every text in **English and Dutch**.

## Småland (Sweden) — summer

*Replaces the earlier dot for Scania.* A summer week in a **red wooden cottage** in Småland, near a lake and the forest. The atmosphere of the classic Swedish children's stories: red houses with white corners, meadows, wooden fences, a jetty on the lake, long light evenings. Inspired by places anyone can visit around **Vimmerby** (Astrid Lindgren's World is a real theme park there).

> **Careful:** Pippi Longstocking and the other Astrid Lindgren characters are copyrighted. Use the *feel* of those stories (red cottages, an adventurous summer, a horse on the veranda as a wink at most), but no names, characters, looks or story lines from the books. Everything stays our own work, as with the other regions.

Ideas:
- **Places:** the red cottage by the lake, a forest with blueberries and mushrooms, Vimmerby and its wooden old town, a glass works (Småland's "Kingdom of Crystal"), a canoe trip on the lake, a Midsummer meadow with a maypole, an old farm with a horse, going home.
- **Summer** like Dalmatia (`summer: true`), but a softer northern summer: green meadows and birch trees instead of limestone; a new ground type (grass and moss, with ant hills or nettles as spikes?).
- **Creatures:** an **elk (moose)**, big and slow, maybe one you cannot jump on but have to avoid; mosquitoes; a fox.
- **Gameplay ideas:**
  - **canoes** on the lake (movers);
  - swimming in the lake (`swim`), cold but fine in summer;
  - **picking blueberries** to collect;
  - a **Midsummer** level with a maypole, flower wreaths and dancing (no enemies, a calm and happy day);
  - the **white summer night**: it hardly gets dark, a light evening sky.
- **Decorations:** red cottages with white corners, wooden fences, birch trees, a maypole, a jetty with a rowing boat, a Dala horse (the red wooden horse), glass objects, a mailbox on a post, hay racks.
- **Food for collectables and jokes:** cinnamon buns (kanelbullar), meatballs with lingonberries, fika (coffee for the others, **tea for Paul and Laura**), strawberries, ice cream.
- **Flag:** blue with a yellow cross.

## The other dots

Short notes to start from; add ideas here when they come up.

- **Iceland:** geysers, waterfalls, black beaches, puffins, northern lights, hot springs (warm water to swim in, in winter).
- **Tuscany:** cypress lanes, vineyards, hill towns, olive groves; summer or autumn.
- **Veneto:** Venice with canals and gondolas (movers), bridges, carnival masks; Lake Garda.
- **Scotland:** highlands, castles, lochs, bagpipes, sheep and Highland cattle, rain and wind.
- **Catalonia:** beaches, Barcelona's colourful buildings, mountain monasteries; summer.
- **Ardèche:** canoeing through the gorge, the stone arch over the river, caves, campings; summer.
- **Greece:** white houses with blue doors, islands, olive trees, ancient temples; summer.
